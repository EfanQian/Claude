INSTRUCTIONS

Controls

Player 1:

Movement: W (Jump), A (Left), S (Crouch), D (Right)

Attacks: J (Light Punch), K (Heavy Punch), L (Kick), U (Special Move)

Player 2:

Movement: Arrow Up (Jump), Left (Left), Down (Crouch), Right (Right)

Attacks: Numpad 1 (Light Punch), Numpad 2 (Heavy Punch), Numpad 3 (Kick), Numpad 4 (Special Move)

Gameplay

• Each player starts with 3 lives

• Reduce opponent's health bar to zero to win a round

• First player to lose all lives loses the match

• Perform combos by chaining attacks together

• Special moves consume energy and have cooldowns

Win Condition

Be the last player standing with lives remaining!
