INSTRUCTIONS

Team Battle Mode

• Each player selects a team of 3 fighters

• When a fighter's health reaches zero, they're defeated and the next fighter comes in

• First player to lose all 3 fighters loses the match!

Controls

Player 1:

Movement: W (Jump), A (Left), S (Crouch), D (Right)

Attacks: Q (Light Punch), E (Heavy Punch), R (Kick), F (Special Move)

Player 2:

Movement: Arrow Up (Jump), Left (Left), Down (Crouch), Right (Right)

Attacks: I (Light Punch), O (Heavy Punch), P (Kick), [ (Special Move)

Gameplay

• Round timer: 99 seconds

• All attacks have a 1-second cooldown

• Build energy by landing successful hits (+10 energy per hit)

• Missing attacks reduces energy (-10 energy per miss)

• Special moves require FULL energy bar (100) and consume all energy

• Perform combos by chaining attacks together for bonus damage

• Get close to your opponent to land attacks

Win Condition

Defeat all 3 of your opponent's fighters to win!
